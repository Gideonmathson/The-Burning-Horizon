<section class="wrap page-head"><h1>Contribute</h1><p><em>The Burning Horizon</em> is designed for multiple authors and multiple forms of writing.</p></section><article class="article"><p>Possible contributions include phenomenological essays, close readings, short fieldnotes, visual or sonic observations, accounts of thermal environments, reports on current heat events, reading notes, translations, interviews and fragments.</p><div class="notice"><strong>Editorial principle:</strong> a piece does not have to be “about climate change” to belong here. It should make something about heat, perception, embodiment or worldhood newly describable.</div><h2>Suggested lengths</h2><p><strong>Fragment:</strong> 100–400 words<br><strong>Fieldnote:</strong> 500–1,500 words<br><strong>Reading:</strong> 800–2,000 words<br><strong>Essay:</strong> 1,500–5,000 words</p><p>For now this page contains no public email address. Add one when you are ready to receive submissions.</p></article>
