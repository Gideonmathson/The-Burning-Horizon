*Sample essay.*

A hot room is not geometrically different from a cool room. Its walls have not moved. The bed remains where it was. Yet the room may become smaller in practice because some positions are no longer tolerable, or larger because sleep requires repeated movement between floor, doorway and window.

This distinction matters for Merleau-Ponty. Lived space is not a container occupied by a body-object. It is articulated through the body's powers and projects.
