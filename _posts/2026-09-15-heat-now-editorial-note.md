This section is meant for current reports, scientific findings, heat action plans, labour disputes, power failures, school closures, night-time heat and other events whose thermal significance is unfolding in public.

The aim is not to convert news into philosophy. Nor is it to decorate phenomenology with topical examples. Instead, each report can be read at several scales: measurement, institution, body, place, duration and experience.
