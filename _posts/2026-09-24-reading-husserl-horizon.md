*Sample reading note.*

The horizon is one of those phenomenological concepts whose apparent abstraction disappears once one begins describing ordinary situations. What is given is never exhausted by what is explicitly present. The cup shows one side and promises others. The street corner is seen together with what might lie beyond it.

Heat sharpens the temporal dimension of this structure. The body anticipates exposure before entering sunlight. It remembers the air-conditioned shop before seeing it. It crosses a road not simply toward a location but toward an expected thermal difference.
