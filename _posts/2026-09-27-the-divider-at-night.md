*Sample fieldnote.*

The concrete divider is not cooler in any simple sense. It has absorbed the day and gives some of it back after sunset. But traffic keeps the air moving. What would count, in a transport plan, as a hostile place for a body can become preferable to a recessed pavement where the air does not move at all.

A phenomenological description has to resist deciding too quickly what the divider *is*. Barrier, bed, danger, refuge, hot surface, breeze corridor: these are not competing metaphors laid over an inert object. They are different ways the object enters a practical world.
